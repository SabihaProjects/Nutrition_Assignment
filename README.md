# Nutrition Assignment: Data-Driven Nutrition Research

## Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive, data-driven research study focused on micronutrient deficiencies in children and nutrition-related health inequalities. It combines data analysis, statistical modelling, evidence synthesis, and sensitivity assessment to explore nutrition indicators, trends, and disparities, with a particular focus on the Indian context.

The project aims to transform nutrition-related data into meaningful insights through structured analytical methods, systematic data processing, and research-oriented interpretation.

## Objectives
- Explore and analyse nutrition-related datasets.
- Assess data quality, missing values, and consistency across sources.
- Harmonise nutrition indicators, age groups, and measurements for comparability.
- Apply statistical estimation techniques to interpret available evidence.
- Conduct sensitivity analysis to examine the robustness of analytical results.
- Investigate nutrition-related trends, inequalities, and regional differences.
- Develop evidence-based insights relevant to public health and nutrition research.

## Methodology
The project follows a structured analytical workflow involving data cleaning, data harmonisation, statistical estimation, uncertainty assessment, sensitivity analysis, visualisation, and evidence synthesis. Multiple analytical scenarios are explored to understand how methodological assumptions may influence the final estimates and interpretations.

## Technical Approach
- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
- Data Validation & Quality Assessment
- Data Harmonisation
- Generalized Least Squares (GLS)
- Inverse-Variance Weighting
- Statistical Estimation & Uncertainty Analysis
- Sensitivity Analysis across multiple scenarios
- Comparative Data Visualisation
- Research Evidence Synthesis

## Tools & Technologies
**Programming:** Python  
**Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib  
**Methods:** Statistical Modelling, Data Analysis, Sensitivity Analysis, Evidence Synthesis

## Research Focus
Micronutrient deficiencies in children, nutrition indicators, deficiency trends, socioeconomic inequalities, regional variation, and nutrition-related evidence in India.

## Key Learning Outcomes
This project demonstrates practical application of data analytical techniques, statistical reasoning, data harmonisation, interpretation of research evidence, and evaluation of methodological assumptions in a real-world public health research context.

## Disclaimer
This repository is intended for academic, analytical, and research purposes. Findings and estimates should be interpreted in the context of the underlying data, sources, and methodological assumptions.
